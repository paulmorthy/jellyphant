Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy covers the Jellyphant website and Jellyphant uploader, an internal tool used only by the Jellyphant team to publish our own videos to our own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok accounts.

Who we are

Jellyphant, Czech Republic. Contact: jellyphanttv@gmail.com.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect any personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs under its own privacy statement.

Jellyphant uploader

The uploader is not offered to the public. It signs in only to accounts owned by Jellyphant and uses the access we grant it to:

  • upload our own videos, thumbnails and subtitles,
  • set titles, descriptions, publishing time and audience settings (e.g. "made for kids"),
  • read basic information about our own channels and accounts (name and ID) to make sure it posts to the right one.

It does not access, collect or store data about any other users, viewers or subscribers. Access tokens are stored only on the Jellyphant team's own computer, are never shared with third parties and can be revoked at any time in the account settings of each platform (e.g. Google account permissions).

Google API Services

Jellyphant uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The uploader uses the YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Our videos are made for children aged 5+ and their families. We do not collect personal data from children or anyone else. Our videos are marked as "made for kids" where the platform supports it.

Deleting data

We do not hold data about you. If you have any question, write to jellyphanttv@gmail.com.
